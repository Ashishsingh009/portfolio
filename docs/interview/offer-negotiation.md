# Offer negotiation — aiming ~30% higher

A recruiter can coach the ask. The market decides. **Do not fabricate a competing offer.**

## What “30% higher” means

Always talk **total first-year cash + equity**. A 30% base bump with a tiny option grant can be a worse deal than 15% base + refresh.

Break every offer:

| Piece | Ask |
|---|---|
| Base | Monthly in INR or USD, currency, location |
| Bonus | Target %, guaranteed vs discretionary |
| Equity | Units, strike, % fully diluted if they will say, vest, refresh |
| Sign-on | Cash to bridge notice + relocation |
| Benefits | PF, health, visa, device |

India Staff/Principal Android at a well-funded AI startup (2026 public chatter, not a salary survey you paid for): wide band. Remote US/EU contractor rates are not your band unless the offer is actually that entity. Use levels.fyi + peers as a **range**, not a fake quote.

## Script — after the number

“I’m excited. I want this to be easy for both sides. Given 14 years, 100M-user ownership, and the ANR/on-device demos, I’m targeting **{base × 1.30}** base and **{equity × 1.20}** equity, or mix to the same total. What is the path to that without a second loop?”

If they say budget is tight: “Then a sign-on of {gap} over 12 months and a 12-month refresh conversation in writing.”

## What not to say

- A company that did not offer you
- “I will walk” unless you will
- Apologizing for the ask
- Negotiating only base while giving away equity

## Leverage that is real

- Another **real** process in flight (name the stage, not a fake number)
- Scarce combo: Android platform + measured AI demos
- Notice period as a planning fact, not a threat

## If they will not move 30%

Take the written offer, ask for 48 hours, compare total, decide. Walking away from a good Staff Android seat because a blog said “always 30%” is how people miss the window.
